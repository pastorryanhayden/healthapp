## Design Context

### Users
Ryan only. He logs food in the kitchen or after a meal, walks in miles, and a Friday weigh-in. He is in a hurry; the job is to log, not to browse a fitness product.

### Brand Personality
Honest, quiet, ledger. No cheerleading. Empty days fail because the point is to log.

### Aesthetic Direction
Warm dark kitchen-notebook, not gym chrome and not mint wellness. Huge remaining-calorie number, tabular figures, pass green / fail red without screaming. Adaptive: thumb-first on iPhone, keyboard-first on Mac.

### Design Principles
1. Logging food is the first thing on Today.
2. One visual system on phone and laptop — layout changes, personality does not.
3. Calendar tells the truth: fail until logged.
4. Weight is a line toward 205 lb, not a trophy.
5. No invented features (auth, social, barcodes, rings).
